.NET Software Engineer at BairesDev

Develop and maintain robust software applications using the .NET framework to deliver reliable solutions that meet business objectives. This role focuses on building scalable backend systems, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and implementing best practices throughout the software development lifecycle.

What You'll Do:

- Design, develop, and maintain .NET applications and services.

- Write clean, efficient code following .NET best practices and coding standards.

- Collaborate with team members to gather requirements and implement solutions.

- Build and integrate APIs, databases, and third-party services.

- Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.

- Debug and troubleshoot applications to ensure optimal performance and reliability.

What We Are Looking For:

- 3+ years of experience with .NET development.

- Strong knowledge of C# and the .NET framework.

- Experience with web development frameworks such as ASP.NET or .NET Core.

- Familiarity with SQL databases and data modeling concepts.

- Understanding of software development principles and design patterns.

- Advanced level of English.

How we do make your work (and your life) easier:

- 100% remote work (from anywhere).

- Excellent compensation in USD or your local currency if preferred

- Hardware and software setup for you to work from home.

- Flexible hours: create your own schedule.

- Paid parental leaves, vacations, and national holidays.

- Innovative and multicultural work environment: collaborate and learn from the global Top 1% of talent.

- Supportive environment with mentorship, promotions, skill development, and diverse growth opportunities.
